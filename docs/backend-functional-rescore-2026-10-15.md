Quota2 raises the count to 4,096 only under an explicit operator policy and explicit client negotiation; the 32-MiB owner byte limit stays unchanged. Legacy defaults remain 512.
- [Private journal v3 capacity](lifecycle-vault-v3-validation-2026-10-14.json) and its [retained Linux raw result](evidence/lifecycle-vault-v3-linux-capacity-20261014.json): the optimized implementation passed the 960-logical-hop, 21,120-real-encrypted-record experiment, including repeated reopen and historical recovery. The validated vault/test sources match their recorded cutoff; the later `CystClient.quotaVersion` visibility change is explicitly recorded in that report.
- [Independent owner funding review](paid-owner-lifeline-validation-2026-10-15.json): 12/12 focused checks and 1/1 actual local Anvil funding acceptance passed. The owner actor preserves exact quote input, conservative cumulative approval, attempted-before-submit records, external durable wallet lookup/anchor, stale funded inventory and admission replay. Actual quota2 clients are checked independently; count/byte shortage and lifecycle/expiry changes during a delayed preflight prevent wallet submission. The Anvil case opens one actual Vault reservation and restores its private journals; it does not dispatch CPU.
- [Mounted CPU-job validation](paid-cpu-focused-validation-2026-10-15.json) and [raw result](evidence/paid-cpu-focused-20261015.json): 37/37 passed. The new paid-job case uses actual local child processes, HTTP, encrypted application checkpoint/restore, host preemption and local Anvil source/target settlement. It rejects replacement while the original refund remains pending, requires the exact owner decision, reuses quotes after lost responses, restores the approved target and reports both lane prices. This run used an explicitly marked child-process fixture, not Docker/gVisor. Its source inventory was captured after execution; it is not a frozen full release acceptance.

The reviewer read the mounted job state machine, route authorization, workload/lane binding, exact replacement approval, canonical liability/cleanup checks and HostKit consent sequencing. No new production financial or lane-consent defect was found in this bounded review. That statement is not an audit certificate or a claim that untested races cannot exist.

## Concrete work still required

1. **External supplier execution and treasury:** enforce a real supplier lease's complete lifetime liability, accept authenticated in-Cell heartbeat consumption, reconcile cancellation/final settlement, implement operational network-token inventory and conservative conversion/rebalancing, and credit finalized external-network top-ups. The current safe refusal of a paid supplier request is not completed supply.
2. **Sealed CPU admission and recovery:** obtain and accept operational TDX/DCAP appraisal, bind the CPU workload and recovery recipient to a fresh challenge, release the key through that verified path and restore inside the actual sealed Cell. Fresh managed model inference also needs an accepted current policy and a verified completion. The expired policy remains blocked.
3. **Persistence and long-lived operation:** general filesystem/state packaging and larger paid restore, the original under-20-second target for up to 2 GiB, independent object-storage failure domains, lost physical-source recovery, and a sustained owner-loop acceptance with real renewed compute leases. Current paid captures remain bounded to 4,096 bytes and leases to 120 seconds. The finite approval lasts at most one day and never renews its own budget; refunds do not replenish it.
4. **Broader scheduling:** jobs are bounded to 10–120 seconds per reservation and at most 64 linked runs, over at most 16 reviewed CPU hosts. One configured offer per physical host, explicit owner approval/funding/restore actions and controller-local observed history are narrower than an autonomous multi-class market. Larger estimated workloads/calibration, continuous event-driven replacement composition, external supplier capacity and broader admitted class/lane price coverage remain. No absent GPU Cell is silently counted as mandatory execution evidence.
5. **Payment, economics and governance:** exact x402-to-canonical-Vault ingress and Selection income/subsidy classification already exist. The missing original payment paths are exact/upto funding and admission of compute under its Leash, Base Account spend-permission consumption and finalized Solana Pay-to-Base credit. Operationalize accepted observation collectors, weekly work roots and bounded buy/burn with explicit operator policies; complete prescribed penalty governance, canonical Base identity/reputation targets and reviewed roles.
6. **Independent launch gates:** exact-source full regression/build and rollout/recovery acceptance, funded Base acceptance when the wallet is supplied, verified public contract sources, independent escrow/registry audits, approved provider appraisals and public operator monitoring. Local Anvil movements and local test signatures do not satisfy these gates.

Unchanged M6/M7 impose the same mathematical limit: even with every other mandatory milestone at 100%, **(700 + 20 + 35) / 9 = 83.888…%**. A defensible 90% claim still needs meaningful supplier and sealed-CPU completion. Removing optional GPU Cells or accumulating more local tests does not change that denominator or remove those requirements.
